Thomas Lloyd — PhD Candidate in Economics, University of Michigan

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Michigan. My research interests span Public and Development Economics with a focus on education, inequality, externalities, and the design and evaluation of public policies.

I previously worked as a research assistant at CREST, the Department of Economics of Ecole Polytechnique and ENSAE. I hold master's degrees from Ecole Polytechnique and HEC Paris, and completed my undergraduate degree at the Toulouse School of Economics.

In September 2026, I will join the Department of Quantitative Finance & Economics at emlyon business school as an Assistant Professor.
