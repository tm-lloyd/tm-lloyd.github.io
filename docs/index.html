Thomas Lloyd — Assistant Professor of Economics at emlyon business school

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Quantitative Finance & Economics at emlyon business school. My research interests span Public and Development Economics with a focus on education, inequality, externalities, and the design and evaluation of public policies.

I obtained my PhD in Economics from the University of Michigan in 2026. I previously worked as a research assistant at CREST, the Department of Economics of Ecole Polytechnique and ENSAE. I hold master's degrees from Ecole Polytechnique and HEC Paris, and completed my undergraduate degree at the Toulouse School of Economics.
